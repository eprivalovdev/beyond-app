Beyond+ subscription

  • Title: Beyond+ unlocks the journeys Beyond the Northern Pass, Iceland Ring Road, Camino de Santiago and Paris to Rome.
  • Price and length: $4.99 per month, or $29.99 per year. Prices are in US dollars and may vary by country.

  • Payment: payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase (or when the trial ends).
  • Auto-renewal: the subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period, at the price of that plan.
  • Managing and cancelling: manage your subscription or turn off auto-renew in your Apple Account settings (iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions) after purchase. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
  • The two free journeys, The Lantern Road and To the Last Lighthouse, stay available without a subscription.
