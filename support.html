Beyond Support

Beyond turns the distance you walk and run into a journey through drawn worlds. Here is how it works, and what to do when something looks off.

How Beyond works

How does my progress work?

Beyond reads your walking and running distance from Apple Health. Every kilometre (or mile) you cover moves a small silhouette traveler along the journey you picked. Chapters, places, encounters, true facts and postcards unlock as you pass them, and everything you unlock stays in your journal so you can read it again later.

How do I switch to a different journey?

Open the journeys list, pick a journey and start it. Journeys marked Beyond+ need a subscription.

Which Health permission does Beyond need, and how do I change it?

Beyond asks to read your walking and running distance. It also reads your step count, only to show it in your walking stats. Beyond never writes anything to Health.

To review or change access: open the Health app, tap your profile picture, choose Apps (under Privacy), then Beyond. You can also go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Beyond. If you turn distance off, the traveler stops moving until you turn it back on.

Why is my distance late or not moving?
  • Beyond shows what Apple Health has recorded. iPhone and Apple Watch sometimes sync to Health in batches, so new distance can appear after a delay.
  • Open Beyond again after a walk to refresh it.
  • Check that distance access is on (see the Health question above).
  • Health needs your iPhone (or an Apple Watch) with you to record distance in the first place.
  • If distance shows in Health but not in Beyond, force-quit and reopen the app.
Can I use miles instead of kilometres?

Yes. Tap the gear in the top-left corner of the journey screen and switch Units between kilometres and miles.

How do the widgets work?

Beyond has Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that show where your traveler is on the current journey. Touch and hold your Home Screen (or Lock Screen), tap the add (+) button, search for Beyond and choose a size. Widgets refresh when the system allows it, so they can lag slightly behind the app.

How do notifications work?

Notifications are optional and are created on your iPhone; nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them on or off in Beyond (the gear in the top-left corner), or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Beyond.

How does Beyond+ work?

Beyond+ is an auto-renewable subscription that unlocks Beyond the Northern Pass, Iceland Ring Road, Camino de Santiago and Paris to Rome.

  • $4.99 per month (no free trial), or $29.99 per year with a 7-day free trial. Prices are in US dollars and may differ by country.
  • If you start the yearly trial, you are charged $29.99 when the 7 days end unless you cancel at least 24 hours before.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
How do I cancel my subscription or trial?

Cancelling is done through Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Beyond+ and tap Cancel (or Cancel Free Trial). You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for or the end of the trial. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Beyond and tap Restore Purchases on the Beyond+ screen or in Settings. Journey progress is stored only on the device, so a new iPhone starts fresh unless you restore a full device backup.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles billing and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Apple Support.

Does Beyond work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Beyond runs on iPhone and on iPhone Duo, and requires iOS 26 or later.

Do I need an account? Where is my data?

No account, no sign-in. Your journey progress and journal stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
