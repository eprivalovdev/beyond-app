Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Short version: Beyond does not collect your data. Everything stays on your iPhone.

This policy covers the Beyond – Miles of Stories iPhone app, published by Egor Privalov, an individual developer ("I", "me").

Apple Health data

  • Beyond asks permission to read your walking and running distance from Apple Health. It also reads your step count, only to show it in your walking stats.
  • Access is read-only. Beyond never writes to Apple Health.
  • This data is processed only on your device to move your traveler along a journey and to draw your stats. It never leaves your iPhone.
  • I do not share, sell or disclose Health data to anyone, and it is never used for advertising, marketing or data-mining purposes.
  • You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app (profile picture → Apps → Beyond) or in iOS Settings.

What I collect

Nothing. I run no servers for the app and receive no data from it. Beyond has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

What is stored on your device

Your journey progress, journal entries, settings (such as units) and the data the widgets display are stored locally in the app's container on your iPhone, including a shared container so the widgets can read them. This data is removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple may include this app data in your device backup under Apple's own terms.

Notifications

Beyond can send optional local notifications. They are scheduled on your device; no push server is involved. You can disable them at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Beyond+ subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes your payment and billing details; I never see your card or payment information.

Children

Beyond is not directed to children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date above.
